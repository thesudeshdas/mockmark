1 Why Review Signal?

Selection corners, numbered remarks, path labels, and a review rail come from shipped behavior. Cool surfaces and signal yellow avoid Claude-coded warmth.

01 / Idea

Mark the work. Keep the context.

Mockmark looks like a precise review instrument: direct enough for people, exact enough for repositories, restrained enough for long review sessions.

01Mark the work

Accent belongs to selection, attention, and change—not decoration.

02Show context

Page path, build, author, time, and status remain visible when useful.

03Neutral + exact

White and cool gray surfaces meet rules, alignment, and explicit labels.

04Human → agent

Conversation is proportional; paths, code, and tokens turn mono.

05Restraint

Few radii, one mark color, quiet elevation, purposeful motion.

Recognizable without a gradient—or even the logo.

Cool canvas, white work surface, signal-yellow mark, open corners, ruled rail, numbered anchors, and proportional-to-mono type form the signature.

08 / Motion

Explain state. Then stop.

Motion reveals origin, continuity, or completion. Product transitions finish within 220ms; emphasis finishes within 360ms.

Place mark140ms / scale + fade
Draw relation220ms / origin left
Open rail220ms / 16px max
✓Confirm state360ms max / once

Reduced motion removes movement and preserves instant visible state. No endless orbit, shimmer, parallax, blob morph, bounce, or type-axis loop.

09 / Rules

Repeat the system, not a trend.

A designer or engineer should know what to keep and what to reject without inventing a new visual language each time.

Do

  • Frame selected work with open corners.
  • Use ink for primary actions and signal yellow for marks.
  • Put paths, builds, shortcuts, and code in mono.
  • Prefer rules and whitespace to nested cards.
  • Use realistic comments, paths, and statuses.
  • Pair status color with text and shape.

Don't

  • Use beige/cream foundations or coral/orange accents.
  • Use purple gradients, glows, blobs, or glass.
  • Center a fake prompt composer or default to bento cards.
  • Make every control or label a pill.
  • Depict agents with robots, wands, or sparkles.
  • Fabricate product features, claims, or screenshots.
